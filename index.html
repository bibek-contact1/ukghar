Who we are

Built by students, for London students

UKGhar means “UK home”: ghar is the word for home. We started in 2019 because finding a decent, safe room near campus was far harder than it should be.

Today we run 10 well-kept properties near London's leading universities. Every room is furnished, every bill is included, and there's always a friendly face on hand to help, so you can focus on your studies and enjoy university life.
