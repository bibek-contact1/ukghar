Privacy

How we use your details

Short version: we only use what you send us to answer your enquiry and help you find a room. We don't sell or share your details for marketing.

Who we are

What we collect and why

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address, university, preferred room type, preferred viewing date and message. We use these to reply to you, arrange viewings and help you book a room. The form sends an email to us; nothing is stored on this website.
  • Room alerts & event news: your email address, used only to send you room availability and event updates. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or contacting us.
  • Phone, WhatsApp and email: if you contact us directly, we keep the conversation so we can follow up on your enquiry.

Who sees your details

Only the UKGhar team. We don't sell your details or pass them to third parties for their own marketing. We share them with the property you're interested in only when you ask us to book a viewing or a room there.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email and we'll sort it out. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and other services

  • This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your cookie choice is remembered in your browser only.
  • The map on the Contact page is provided by Google Maps, and the site's font by Google Fonts. When those load, Google may receive your IP address and set its own cookies, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If we change how we use your details, we'll update this page. Last updated: 11 November 2026.
